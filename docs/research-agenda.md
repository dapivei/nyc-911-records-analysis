# Research Agenda

The current implemented analysis focuses on DEP inspection, testing, cleanup, and clearance records. The following questions are broader hypotheses for future work; they should not be read as findings established by the current pipeline.

## Network Topology

How did inter-agency communication networks operate?

**Hypothesis:** Municipal regulatory agencies and private real-estate entities may exhibit higher betweenness and degree centrality than public-health advocacy groups.

## Spatial Prioritization

How were Lower Manhattan blocks prioritized for testing?

**Hypothesis:** Commercial hubs may show a concentration of earlier testing records compared with residential zones.

## Sentiment Over Time

How did administrative language about liability and risk change relative to public-facing messaging?

**Hypothesis:** Internal records may show an earlier increase in liability- and risk-related terminology than public guidance.
