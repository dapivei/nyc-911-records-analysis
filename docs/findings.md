# Findings: DEP Oversight Coverage

These findings summarize the current page-level analysis of the released DEP records. They describe the archive, not all post-9/11 testing or cleanup activity. In particular, a report not found in DEP's released files is not proof that testing did not happen through another agency or program. See [Methodology and Archival Forensics](methodology.md) for classification and date limitations.

## Coverage

The analysis covers 941 Lower Manhattan buildings. Of these, 727 (77.3%) have a documented DEP inspection record; 554 have environmental test reports; 262 have exterior cleanup records; 105 have interior cleanup records; and 83 have clearance records.

Among the 727 buildings with a documented inspection, 377 (51.9%) have at least one matched environmental test report in the released records, while 350 (48.1%) have no matched test report in the DEP archive.

The 554 count is the total buildings with a test report in the analysis universe; 377 is the subset of inspected buildings that also have a test report.

## Test-Report Coverage by Distance

Among 719 inspected buildings with valid coordinates, report coverage varies by distance from the World Trade Center site. The reported chi-square test finds an association between distance band and test-report coverage (`p < 0.001`). The nearest bands contain small samples, so interpret their percentages alongside the counts.

| Distance from WTC site | Inspected | With report | Without report | Share with report |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| < 250 m | 8 | 7 | 1 | 87.5% |
| 250–500 m | 119 | 99 | 20 | 83.2% |
| 500–750 m | 256 | 131 | 125 | 51.2% |
| 750 m–1 km | 193 | 64 | 129 | 33.2% |
| > 1 km | 143 | 70 | 73 | 49.0% |

## Exterior Cleanup Sequence

Among 69 buildings with verified day-precision dates for both inspection and exterior cleanup, cleanup followed inspection in the expected chronological order 88.4% of the time. The median lag was 124 days (IQR 30–139 days). This result applies only to the subset with usable verified dates.
